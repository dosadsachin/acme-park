SECTION1The problem in my words

The competitor is the parking app already on the driver's phone.

The real problem

Drivers already pay by phone. The car only wins where it knows more: the plate, the spot, and when you park or leave.

Solving for

City regulars who pay for street parking several times a week.

SECTION2Assumptions and questions

What I'd have asked, and what I assumed instead.

Can GPS find the right zone?Mostly. The driver confirms it against the street sign.
Whose account pays?The driver's own ParkMobile account, linked once by QR.
What's allowed while driving?Nothing that moves money. Paying needs Park.
How is revenue split?A share of ParkMobile's fee on in-car sessions.
